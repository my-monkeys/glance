Glance

Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 11, 2026
In short: Glance collects no personal data. Your credentials are stored locally on your device, and the app only communicates with the analytics servers that you configure. No data is ever sent to My-Monkey or to any third party.

Data we collect

None. Glance has no server that would receive your data, contains no third-party analytics or advertising SDK, and performs no tracking.

Data stored on your device

To work, the app keeps the following locally:

  • the credentials of your analytics accounts (URL, token or username/password), stored encrypted in your device's Keychain;
  • your preferences (tracked sites, time range, theme, layout), stored locally.

This information never leaves your device, except to query your own analytics servers (see below).

Network communications

Glance only communicates with the analytics instances you configure (for example your Umami or Plausible server), in order to display your statistics. These exchanges happen directly between your device and your servers, read-only. Glance never modifies your analytics data.

Third-party services

Glance embeds no third-party collection, analytics or advertising service. The only servers it contacts are the ones you configured yourself.

Deleting your data

You can remove an account at any time from the app's settings, or uninstall Glance: all locally stored credentials and preferences are then erased from your device.

Children

Glance is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect any data about them.

Changes

This policy may be updated. The date of the latest update is shown at the top of the page.

Contact

Glance is a project by the My-Monkey collective. For any question about this policy, visit my-monkey.fr.
