FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What is Glance?

Glance is a free, open source mobile and desktop client that displays your Umami and Plausible statistics. It brings all your sites together in one clean app — smooth charts, real-time and widgets — without ever leaving your analytics instance.

Is Glance free?

Yes, Glance is completely free and open source (MIT license). No account to create, no subscription, no site limit: install the app and connect your Umami or Plausible instances.

Which analytics tools are supported?

Glance connects to Umami and Plausible today, including self-hosted instances. Fathom support is planned. You can track multiple accounts and providers at the same time, brought together in a single view.

Which platforms is Glance available on?

Glance is available on iOS (App Store), Android (APK), macOS (Homebrew or DMG) and Windows. The app is built with Flutter, so it's genuinely cross-platform.

Is my data sent to any third party?

No. Glance talks directly to your Umami or Plausible instance, read-only, with no middleman server and no tracking. Your credentials stay encrypted in your device's keychain.

Does Glance work with a self-hosted instance?

Yes. Whether your Umami or Plausible instance is self-hosted or cloud, Glance connects to it directly with your URL and API key. No server-side configuration is needed.
