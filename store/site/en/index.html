Your web stats,

Glance is the mobile and desktop client for Umami and Plausible. All your sites at a glance, plus real dashboards to understand what is happening on them.

Compare sites

Compare your sites

Several sites side by side: one curve per site, plus the total. From 1 to 6 rows, from one or several Umami accounts.

Add a “from” or “excluding visitors from” filter on a domain to see what your sites send each other.

A separate view adds up the visitors your sites send each other.

Events

Track every event

One curve and one color per event, filterable by property: plan = pro, amount ≥ 20.

Count, compute a rate, and look at the breakdown of a property’s values and how it evolves over time.

Funnel

See where they drop off

From 2 to 8 steps, with the number of visitors and the drop-off at every step.

You choose the delay between two steps. Umami only.

Retention

Measure retention

Plug in a metrics source: a small API on your site, protected by a token.

Glance then shows D1, D7 and D30 retention, accounts created, any indicator your site exposes. The computation stays on your own database.

Read the metrics source documentation

Forecast

See the month’s end coming

On “this month” and “this year”, your curve continues as an orange dotted line, with the expected total.

The forecast starts from the last 7 days’ level and the weekly rhythm.

Live and widgets

Live, right on your home screen

Who is on each site right now, sorted by activity.

iOS and Android widgets for your totals or for a specific site.

Every device

On all your devices

iPhone, iPad, Android, Mac and Windows, in light and dark. On Mac and Windows, the site list sits on the left and the detail on the right.

An encrypted QR code to pick up your setup in one scan, or end-to-end encrypted sync between your devices (optional, one-time purchase).

Frequently asked questions

What is Glance?

Glance is a free, open source mobile and desktop client that displays your Umami and Plausible statistics. It brings all your sites together in one clean app — smooth charts, real-time and widgets — without ever leaving your analytics instance.

Is Glance free?

Yes: the app is free and open source (MIT license), with no account to create, no subscription and no site limit. Only one thing is paid, and it's optional — Glance Sync, the encrypted backup of your setup — as a one-time purchase.

Which analytics tools are supported?

Glance connects to Umami and Plausible today, including self-hosted instances. Fathom support is planned. You can track multiple accounts and providers at the same time, brought together in a single view.

Which platforms is Glance available on?

Glance is available on iOS (App Store), Android (Play Store), macOS (Homebrew or DMG) and Windows. The app is built with Flutter, so it's genuinely cross-platform.

Is my data sent to any third party?

No. Glance talks directly to your Umami or Plausible instance, read-only, with no middleman server and no tracking. Your credentials stay encrypted in your device's keychain.

What is Glance Sync?

A paid add-on (one-time purchase) that backs up your accounts and groups and restores them on your other devices. Your setup is encrypted on-device before upload: the server only ever stores an unreadable blob. Without it Glance runs entirely locally, and QR transfer stays free.

Can I split my sites by client or project?

Yes, with groups. A group is a reading scope: totals, charts and live counts only include its sites. You switch between groups from the home screen.

Does Glance work with a self-hosted instance?

Yes. Whether your Umami or Plausible instance is self-hosted or cloud, Glance connects to it directly with your URL and API key. No server-side configuration is needed.
